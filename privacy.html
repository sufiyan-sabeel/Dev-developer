Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Template notice. This policy was written by inspecting the site’s own code, and it describes only what this site actually does. It is a starting template, not legal advice — review it (and update it whenever you add a tool) before relying on it.

DEV DEVELOPER is a personal portfolio for Umaiz Sufiyan, published as a static site on GitHub Pages. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no contact forms, no payments and no server-side database. This page explains the small amount of data that is involved when you visit.

1. What is stored in your browser

The site uses your browser’s local storage (not cookies) for two things only:

  • devdev_theme — remembers which colour theme you picked (Graphite Blue, Many Color, Monochrome or Warm Dark).
  • devdev_dashboard_v1 — a five-minute cache of the public GitHub data shown in the dashboard, plus the time it was fetched, so the site does not re-request it constantly.

Both are stored only on your own device. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings, and the site will keep working.

2. Third-party services the site contacts

When you load the site, your browser makes requests to the services below. As with any web request, they receive your IP address and browser user-agent. They are used to run the page, not to identify you.

3. Analytics

Google Analytics 4 is not currently active on this site — no measurement ID is configured, so no analytics script is loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If analytics is enabled in future, it will load only after you accept cookies through the consent banner, and this policy will be updated.

4. Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. If you accept analytics in future, Google Analytics may set its own cookies for measurement.

5. Links to other sites

The site links to external destinations such as GitHub and Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them.

6. Your choices

  • Clear local storage in your browser to remove the saved theme and cache.
  • Block third-party requests or use a content blocker; the core content of the portfolio still works.
  • Decline the analytics banner, if it is ever shown.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach out through github.com/sufiyan-sabeel.
