THE KAGEOS AMBITION

Building toward something bigger.

KageOS represents the overarching creative umbrella for my developer journey — a personal ecosystem where experimental ideas, utilities, and software concepts are incubated.

At the center of this ambition is AIRIS-CLI (Artificial Intelligence Responsive Integrated System). My goal is to evolve AIRIS-CLI into one of the world's most accessible, robust open-source AI coding and automation platforms.

FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions, answered.

Short answers to what people usually ask. Anything not covered here is best asked directly.

Who are you, and what do you build?

I’m Umaiz Sufiyan, a student developer and AI builder. I work on open-source developer tooling, Android apps and web interfaces, and my main focus is AIRIS-CLI, an AI coding and automation CLI.

What is AIRIS-CLI?

AIRIS-CLI stands for Artificial Intelligence Responsive Integrated System — a command-line assistant for coding, automation and intelligent workflows. My goal is to evolve it into an accessible, robust open-source AI coding and automation platform.

What is KageOS?

KageOS is the umbrella for my developer journey — a personal ecosystem where experimental ideas, utilities and software concepts are incubated. AIRIS-CLI sits at the centre of it.

What do you work with?

Mostly Kotlin, TypeScript/JavaScript and Python, across AI and CLI tooling, Android apps, and web and 3D front-end work. The live dashboard shows the exact language breakdown across my public repositories.

How does the live dashboard work?

It reads my public repositories from the GitHub REST API in your browser, computes the totals from that response, and caches it for a few minutes. If GitHub is unavailable it keeps showing the last loaded data and says so — nothing on it is invented.

Are you open to collaboration?

Yes — I’m always happy to connect with other builders, collaborate on open-source initiatives, or talk about AI and developer tooling. The quickest route is through GitHub.
